Integrating AI with Smart Devices (AI on Edge)

Your most personal data belongs in your head and on your wrist-not in the cloud."
This is the core of our Edge Al project. We are moving away from scattered apps that spy on you and moving toward a single, private "brain" that integrates your fitness, your mental health, and your learning into one seamless experience.
Today, our technology is fragmented. You have a watch for your heart, a phone for your mood, and an app for your skills. These tools don't talk to each other, and worse, they send your most
intimate biometrics-your voice, your face, and your vitals-to distant servers

Main Goal: To develop a decentralized, multi-modal Edge AI framework that provides real-time insights into a user's physical health, cognitive skills, and emotional well-being while maintaining total data privacy.
Physical Intelligence: Implement a Random Forest Regressor to predict calorie expenditure and monitor workout efficiency directly on wearable hardware.
Cognitive Assessment: Deploy an on-device NLP Scoring Engine to evaluate linguistic proficiency (Cohesion, Syntax, Grammar) for secure, private communication coaching.
Emotional Analysis: Perform Digital Phenotyping by correlating social media usage patterns with emotional states to detect early signs of digital fatigue.
Edge Optimization: Minimize latency and maximize data security by ensuring all sensitive personal metrics remain on the local device.



RESULTS


Latency: Inference speeds achieved between 10ms – 100ms, representing a 75–90% reduction compared to cloudround-trips.

Accuracy: Optimized models maintained 96–98% accuracy (specifically on the WESAD dataset for stress detection).

Privacy: 100% data localization; no raw biometric or audio data was transmitted over the network during operation.

Energy: Power consumption stayed under 2W, enabling full-day battery life for wearable applications.
